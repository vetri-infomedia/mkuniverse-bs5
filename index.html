The Author

Amizhdhan
Manikandan

"Every story begins with a thought, and every thought deserves to be heard."

Born in Ponnamaravathy, Pudukkottai District, Tamil Nadu, Amizhdhan Manikandan is a Tamil author with a passion for Tamil heritage, history, science, and imaginative storytelling.

He holds a Master of Computer Applications (MCA) degree. His unique blend of technology and storytelling inspires him to create worlds where ancient Tamil heritage meets modern imagination.

His debut work, “Seyon,” combines mystery, adventure, science, nature, and Tamil heritage through cinematic visuals and immersive storytelling.

He is also the creator of MK Universe — “Journey Into a New World,” a growing universe of stories, characters, and adventures.

More than a book. An experience. A world waiting to be explored.
